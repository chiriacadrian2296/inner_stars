# Reflection questions: draft (English only)

5 groups per area, 25 questions each. ⭐ = existing question; its index is its
id (`ReflectionAnswer.questionId`) and must not change. Indexes 4–24 are new and
append-only. Group order is for display only.

Numbering scheme per area: group 1 = [0, 4–7], group 2 = [1, 8–11],
group 3 = [2, 12–15], group 4 = [3, 16–19], group 5 = [20–24].

## Physical

**1. Body Now**
- ⭐ [0] How do you feel in your body these days?
- [4] Where do you hold tension, and when did you last notice it?
- [5] How well did you sleep this week, and what affected it?
- [6] What is your body asking for that you keep ignoring?
- [7] How would you describe your relationship with your body right now?

**2. Care & Health**
- ⭐ [1] What do you do regularly to take care of your health?
- [8] What check-up or appointment have you been postponing?
- [9] What does your usual diet give you, and what does it take away?
- [10] How much water, rest and daily movement do you actually get?
- [11] What is one small thing that would make your daily care easier?

**3. Habits & Movement**
- ⭐ [2] What's a physical habit you'd like to build, and what's holding you back?
- [12] What kind of movement do you genuinely enjoy?
- [13] What habit do you do without thinking that you'd like to change?
- [14] What makes you skip exercise, and what has helped before?
- [15] What would a realistic week of movement look like for you?

**4. Energy**
- ⭐ [3] When do you feel most energetic during the day? What brings that on?
- [16] What drains your energy the most?
- [17] What leaves you feeling recharged after a tiring day?
- [18] How do you usually treat tiredness: push through it or listen to it?
- [19] When did you last feel strong in your body? What were you doing?

**5. Strength & Future**
- [20] What would you like your body to be able to do in ten years?
- [21] What physical limit have you accepted, and which one are you ready to question?
- [22] What are you grateful your body does for you every day?
- [23] Who or what helps you stay active?
- [24] What would taking your body seriously look like this month?

## Psychological

**1. Right Now**
- ⭐ [0] What's weighing on your mind the most right now?
- [4] What emotion have you felt most often this week?
- [5] What are you avoiding thinking about?
- [6] If your mind were a weather report today, what would it say?
- [7] What do you need more of right now, and what do you need less of?

**2. Stress & Coping**
- ⭐ [1] How do you handle stress when it shows up?
- [8] What are the first signs that you're getting overwhelmed?
- [9] What do you do to cope that helps in the moment but hurts later?
- [10] Who or what helps you recover when things get hard?
- [11] What's one situation you could stop reacting to and start choosing a response for?

**3. Thoughts & Patterns**
- ⭐ [2] What recurring thought would you like to let go of?
- [12] What story do you keep telling yourself about who you are?
- [13] When do you tend to be hardest on yourself?
- [14] What would you say to a friend who was thinking what you're thinking?
- [15] What belief about yourself has changed in the last few years?

**4. Calm & Resilience**
- ⭐ [3] What makes you feel calm and centered?
- [16] When was the last time you felt truly at peace? What was around you?
- [17] What have you gotten through that you once thought you couldn't?
- [18] What small ritual could you add to your day to feel more grounded?
- [19] What does rest actually look like for you, not just stopping work?

**5. Growth & Care**
- [20] What would you like to understand better about yourself?
- [21] What would it mean to be kinder to yourself this month?
- [22] What kind of support would you like, and who could you ask?
- [23] Which emotion do you find hardest to sit with, and why?
- [24] What would your mind look like if it felt a little lighter?

## Professional

**1. Fulfillment**
- ⭐ [0] Do you feel fulfilled in your work? Why or why not?
- [4] Which part of your work do you lose track of time in?
- [5] What part of your work would you happily stop doing?
- [6] What do you tell people about your job, and is it what you feel?
- [7] On a good workday, what happened?

**2. Skills & Learning**
- ⭐ [1] What's the next skill you want to develop?
- [8] What are you better at than most people around you?
- [9] Who could you learn from, and what would you ask them?
- [10] What have you learned recently that surprised you?
- [11] What skill, if you had it, would change what you can do?

**3. Meaning & Impact**
- ⭐ [2] What would make your work feel more meaningful?
- [12] Who benefits from what you do?
- [13] What would you do if money were not a concern?
- [14] Which of your values does your work honour, and which does it ignore?
- [15] What would you want to be remembered for at work?

**4. Direction & Ambition**
- ⭐ [3] Where do you see yourself professionally a year from now?
- [16] What opportunity are you hesitating to go for?
- [17] What does success mean to you, in your own terms?
- [18] What would you change about your role if you could?
- [19] What's the next step you could take this month?

**5. Balance & Environment**
- [20] How does your work affect the rest of your life?
- [21] How do you feel about the people and culture around you at work?
- [22] When did you last say no at work, and what did it cost or give you?
- [23] What boundary around your work would you like to set?
- [24] What would a sustainable pace look like for you?

## Financial

**1. Relationship With Money**
- ⭐ [0] What's your relationship with money like?
- [4] What did you learn about money growing up?
- [5] What emotion comes up when you check your accounts?
- [6] What do you spend on that truly adds value to your life?
- [7] What do you spend on that you'd rather not?

**2. Worries & Risks**
- ⭐ [1] What worries you most about your finances?
- [8] Are you avoiding any financial task, and why?
- [9] How prepared are you for an unexpected expense?
- [10] What debts or obligations weigh on you, and what's the plan?
- [11] What would you want to know about money that you don't yet?

**3. Goals & Plans**
- ⭐ [2] What's one concrete financial goal for the coming months?
- [12] How much do you save regularly, and is it enough for what you want?
- [13] What big purchase or project are you planning toward?
- [14] What habit would improve your finances the most?
- [15] How would you like your income to look in three years?

**4. Security & Freedom**
- ⭐ [3] What would financial security actually feel like for you?
- [16] How much is enough for you?
- [17] What would you do with more financial freedom?
- [18] What does a safety net look like for you?
- [19] Where does your sense of security come from other than money?

**5. Values & Giving**
- [20] What does your spending say about what you value?
- [21] How do you decide what is worth paying for?
- [22] Do you share money with people or causes you care about, and how does it feel?
- [23] What would your future self thank you for doing with money now?
- [24] Who could you talk to openly about money?

## Philanthropic

**1. Contribution**
- ⭐ [0] How do you contribute to something bigger than yourself?
- [4] What do you wish you could change in the world around you?
- [5] What kind of difference would you like to make?
- [6] What are you already doing that matters to others?
- [7] How would your community notice if you weren't there?

**2. Giving & Helping**
- ⭐ [1] Who have you helped recently, and how did it make you feel?
- [8] Who helped you when you needed it?
- [9] What's an act of kindness you remember from a stranger?
- [10] What stops you from helping more?
- [11] What small act of kindness could you do this week?

**3. Causes**
- ⭐ [2] What cause matters to you, and why?
- [12] What injustice makes you angry or sad?
- [13] What organisation or person do you admire for their work?
- [14] What personal experience shapes the cause you care about?
- [15] How could you learn more about this cause?

**4. Resources**
- ⭐ [3] What could you give — time, skills, energy — that you're not giving yet?
- [16] What skill of yours could help a person or group?
- [17] How much time could you realistically give each month?
- [18] What would you give if money were no object?
- [19] Who could you join or support so that you don't have to do it alone?

**5. Legacy & Values**
- [20] What would you like to leave behind for others?
- [21] How do you teach or inspire others by example?
- [22] How do you balance caring for yourself and caring for others?
- [23] What does generosity mean to you beyond money?
- [24] What would a kinder world look like in your corner of it?
